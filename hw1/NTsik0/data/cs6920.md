# CS6920 · Building AI-Powered Applications · Fall 2026

Last updated: Week 2 (sample data, summarized from the public course README on GitHub)

## Overview
CS6920 at Kutaisi International University trains students to work as operators: engineers who direct AI with specifications, delegate work to models and agents, and verify everything with tests and measurements. Lectures are recorded and released at least 48 hours before each lab. Labs are live online, weekly from Week 2, in two groups.

## Instructor and contact
The instructor is Zeshan Ahmad. Announcements are posted on Teams. Office hours are held over Google Meet and booked by email. Ask for help in lab first, on Teams second, and by email third, and always post the exact error message.

## Homework
HW1 "Spec to Ship" is worth 5 points and is individual. HW1 is due Thursday of Week 4 at 23:59 Tbilisi time. Submit HW1 by pushing to hw1/<your-github-username>/ in the team repo and filling the submission form linked on Teams. HW1 contains a one-page spec, the working feature it describes, 3 golden questions with results, a half-page delegation log, and the Pattern Journal for Weeks 1 to 4. HW2 "Tool Contract" is worth 5 points, is released with the Week 5 lab and is due at the end of Week 7.

## Grading
The course is graded out of 100 points. Interim work is worth 70 points with a minimum of 25: the midterm exam 25, the Design Review in Week 4 10, the Safety and Evaluation Audit in Week 11 10, participation 15 (HW1 5, HW2 5, watch-checks 3, peer form 2), and Quiz 1 and Quiz 2 at 5 points each. Final work is worth 30 points with a minimum of 16: Demo Day 20 and the Repository Review 10. The capstone accounts for 50 of the 100 points.

## Exams and quizzes
The midterm exam is on campus during Weeks 8 and 9 and covers Weeks 1 to 7. There are no classes in Weeks 8 and 9. Quiz 1 is in the Week 5 lab and Quiz 2 is in the Week 12 lab. Quizzes and the midterm are AI-free.

## Watch-checks
From Week 3, a 3-question watch-check on the lecture recording is due before each lab. It takes two minutes, is auto-graded, and the best 8 of 10 count.

## Teams
Capstone teams have 2 to 4 people. Teams lock at the end of Week 3 with a signed team contract, TEAM-CONTRACT.md at the team repo root.

## AI use policy
Using AI tools on labs, homework and the capstone is encouraged. Every submission logs what was delegated and how it was verified. Submitting output you cannot explain is misconduct, and an oral defense of any work can be required.

## Demo Day
Demo Day is in Week 15: an investor pitch to industry judges with a live demo of the product. The Repository Review is due the Sunday after Demo Day at 23:59 Tbilisi time.
