## Contribution
Work is visible in commits and the journal. If someone carries or someone coasts, we say it in week terms, not in Week 15.

## When it breaks
Step 1: name it inside the team within 48 hours. Step 2: if unresolved in one week, we bring it to the instructor together. No-show rule: after 2 missed syncs without notice, we email the instructor together.
